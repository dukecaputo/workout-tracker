The 60-Minute Outdoor Road Run

Zone 2 + VO2

Sub for any Day 2 or Day 4 session when you have no gym access.

• First 35–40 min: Steady Zone 2 (Conversational pace; HR 65–75% max).
• Next 15 min (VO2 Max Surges): 5 rounds of: 1 min hard uphill surge + 2 min easy jog.
• Final 5 min: Walking cool-down + nasal box breathing.
